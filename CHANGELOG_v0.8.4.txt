DEJA v0.8.4 — PIN protection and owner-controlled profile reset

Implemented in source:
- Native PIN format validation (6–12 digits).
- Persistent failed-attempt tracking for AILI and MAGGY.
- Escalating pauses: 60 seconds after 5 failures, 5 minutes after 10, and 30 minutes after 15 or more.
- Successful PIN verification clears the failed-attempt counter and lockout.
- Owner-only profile reset, requiring the current owner biometric session.
- Reset removes the selected profile's PIN hash/salt and all saved permissions.
- Re-registration generates a fresh random salt and clears lockout history.
- Updated app version to 0.8.4 (versionCode 10).

Not verified:
- Android compilation and physical-device behavior; no Gradle wrapper/Android SDK build was available in this environment.
- PIN recovery is deliberately owner reset + new registration; there is no PIN reveal or remote recovery.
